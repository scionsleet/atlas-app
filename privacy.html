Privacy Policy

Atlas: Functional Medicine · Updated November 8, 2026

Atlas does not collect any data. The developer has no servers and never receives, sees or stores anything about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your device

The whole library ships inside the app and works offline. The app remembers your recently read entries and your text-zoom setting on your device only. Nothing is synced or uploaded.

What Atlas doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising
  • No tracking, and no data shared with or sold to anyone
  • No health data is read from or written to Apple Health

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored.
